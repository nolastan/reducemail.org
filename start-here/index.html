Start Here

Looking to drastically reduce the amount of postal mail you receive? While there's no single unsubscribe or "do not mail" list, you can tackle a large portion of your unwanted mail by filling out these forms.

💡 Have every address you receive mail at handy — current home, previous addresses, and any name variations mailers use. Each one is usually a separate entry on these lists.

  1. Clear the big lists

    The five forms below cover the brokers and cooperatives that resell your address to thousands of mailers.

  2. Opt out brand by brand

    Work through the opt-out form directory for the senders still writing to you.

  3. Send deletion requests

    For brands without an opt-out form, use a privacy portal to request deletion.
