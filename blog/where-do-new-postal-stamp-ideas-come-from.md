# Where do new postal stamp ideas come from?

There are dozens of petitions online proposing new postal stamp designs.

Thousands of people believe that legendary musicians such as [Tom Petty](https://www.change.org/p/create-forever-stamp-honoring-tom-petty?source_location=decision_maker_profile) and [John Prine](https://www.change.org/p/united-states-postal-service-put-john-prine-on-a-usps-stamp-3e97d3a1-8213-4d03-8dc7-24e3476b08a5?source_location=decision_maker_profile) deserve a commemorative stamp–especially since John Prine once worked for the postal service. On the acting side, there's a call for a [Vincent Price stamp](https://www.change.org/p/united-states-postal-service-put-the-actor-vincent-price-on-a-stamp?source_location=decision_maker_profile).

![](https://reducemail.org/content/images/2023/10/IMG-257-1.jpg)

There's a petition for a [Black Lives Matter](https://www.change.org/p/united-states-postal-service-black-lives-matter-forever-postage-stamp?source_location=decision_maker_profile) stamp and one for honoring [Vivien Thomas, a Black Medical Pioneer](https://www.change.org/p/united-states-postal-service-honor-vivien-thomas-a-pioneering-black-heart-surgeon-with-a-commemorative-usps-stamp?source_location=decision_maker_profile).

Some believe that [Yoga & Meditation deserve a stamp](https://www.change.org/p/join-us-as-we-petition-the-u-s-postal-service-for-a-new-stamp-commemorating-yoga-meditation?source_location=decision_maker_profile), with the idea that this would encourage people to live more fit and healthy lifestyles.

So, are these petitions effective?

## How USPS decides on new stamps

While these petitions are aimed at the USPS generally, it might be more effective to petition the [Citizens’ Stamp Advisory Committee](https://about.usps.com/who/csac/) specifically. This 11-person group appointed by the postmaster general does indeed solicit ideas from the general public – but they only accept suggestions via postal mail. Seems on-brand!

![undefined](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/Louise_Fili_and_Gail_Anderson.jpg/2560px-Louise_Fili_and_Gail_Anderson.jpg)

*[Gail Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gail_Anderson_(graphic_designer)#cite_ref-12) is a graphic designer and educator who serves on the advisory committee. (Photo by [Irina Lee](https://www.flickr.com/photos/aigany/8086724353/))*

Once an idea is submitted for consideration by the committee at its quarterly meeting, it takes at minimum three years for the stamp to become a reality. What exactly happens at these meetings? Seems like we will never know, as they are "closed and confidential."
