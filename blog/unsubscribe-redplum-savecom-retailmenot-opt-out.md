# How to unsubscribe from Save.com, Redplum, and RetailMeNot

Nearly every household in the United States receives junk mail from Save.com. In fact, [The New York Times reported](https://www.nytimes.com/2007/12/27/business/media/27adco.html) back in 2007 that 90% of American households received Redplum, which was rebranded to Retailmenot Everyday and then [rebranded again to Save.com](https://help.retailmenot.com/s/topic/0TO4z000000UkNAGA0/save-previously-retailmenot-everyday).

![](https://reducemail.org/content/images/2023/10/Valassis_World_Headquarters_Livonia_Michigan-1-1.jpg)

*Valassis Headquarters (By Dwight Burdette, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0), via Wikimedia Commons)*

Are you looking to opt-out of these promotional mailers? Save.com makes unsubscribing surprisingly easy. All you have to do is enter your mailing address on their [Delivery Options form](https://save.com/mailing/delivery-options). After six weeks, you should stop receiving mail from Save.com

> [Delivery Options | Save.com](https://save.com/mailing/delivery-options)
> Save.com Logo

Since the mailers are addressed to "Resident" rather than to any particular person, there's no need to enter your name on the form.
