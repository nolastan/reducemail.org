Decoder

Postal acronyms

What do those codes on your mail mean?

ECRWSSEDDM
Extended Carrier Route Walking Sequence Saturation — Every Door Direct Mail
ECRLOT
Enhanced Carrier Route Line of Travel
ECRWSH
Enhanced Carrier Route Walk Sequence High Density

ℹ️ These codes mean the mailer bought a route, not your name. Address-level opt-outs won't stop them — that mail is addressed to the route itself.
