About

Hi, my name is Stanford Rosenthal 👋

Since 2017, I've been on a mission to stop the deluge of paper mail that ends up in my trash. It's been a slow, manual process, but I've succeeded! I created ReduceMail.org to help others rid themselves of physical junk mail.

If you're looking to support this project, please get in touch with me at stan@talktomel.com.

Free, and staying that way
