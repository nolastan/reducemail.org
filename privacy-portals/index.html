Privacy Portals

While these brands don't make it easy to opt out of postal mail, they will respect deletion requests as required by law. Submit a request through the portal and ask them to delete your personal information.

ℹ️ These forms are built for state privacy laws like the CCPA, so they often ask which state you live in. Answer honestly — many companies apply deletion requests nationwide rather than maintain separate processes.

What to ask for

Portals vary, but the request that stops mail is usually labeled "Delete my personal information" or "Do not sell or share my personal information." You'll typically need the mailing address printed on the piece you received, and you may get a confirmation email you have to click.

⚠️ Deletion is broader than an opt-out. If you're an active customer, deleting your data may also close out order history, rewards, or account access. Use these when you're done doing business with the sender.
